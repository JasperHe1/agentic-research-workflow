# Research Intake

## Raw Puzzle

Organizations need inventors to search beyond familiar technological domains, but competitive pressure may encourage both exploration and short-term risk avoidance.

## Why It Is Interesting

The same competitive pressure could increase the need for novel solutions while simultaneously reducing tolerance for uncertain search.

## Initial Research Question

Under what organizational conditions does competitive pressure lead inventors to pursue local rather than distant search?

## Potential Mechanisms

- performance feedback
- risk tolerance
- time horizons
- incentive design

## Empirical or Simulation Possibilities

This public example is intentionally unresolved. A later extension could compare an archival design with a carefully validated agent-based simulation.
